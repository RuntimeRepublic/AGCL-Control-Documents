00M — Lesser Magistrate and Interposition Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Draft (not adopted)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-11-15
Supersedes: none
Scope: All AGCL-governed authorities, lesser magistrates, citizen teams, and correction of higher authority


I. PURPOSE

This Control sets the ordered steps by which citizens and lawful lesser magistrates hold a higher magistrate accountable.

The law is king, not the king the law.
Correction comes by lawful steps, in order, on the public record.
Private persons never take the sword.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Follow the founders' sequence: a long train of documented abuse, facts submitted to a candid world, repeated petition, warning, peaceful pressure, and only then action by a lawful civil body.


III. DEFINITIONS

Higher Magistrate
An authority whose acts are under review.

Lesser Magistrate
A lawfully appointed or elected lower authority acting within its own office: a governor, legislature, county officer, sheriff, or jury.

Team
A voluntary citizen body modeled on the 1772 committees of correspondence (a Runtime Republic Study Circle).

Revere Network
The network that carries confirmed smoke-test failures between teams, modeled on the 1775 post riders.

Interposition
A lesser magistrate standing between citizens and an unlawful act of a higher magistrate, within the lesser magistrate's own lawful powers.


IV. THE LADDER

Each step must be complete and on the public record before the next begins.

Rule M1 — A Long Train, Not Light Causes (teams)
A documented pattern of failed smoke tests over time (00L), not one failure.
"Governments long established should not be changed for light and transient causes" (Declaration of Independence, 1776).

Rule M2 — Facts Submitted to a Candid World (teams; Revere Network)
Every grievance is a cited test card with its failure record on the public ledger (00G).

Rule M3 — Petition, Repeatedly (teams and citizens)
Citizen triggers and petitions to officeholders, with every reply logged.
"We for ten years incessantly and ineffectually besieged the throne as supplicants" (Declaration of the Causes and Necessity of Taking Up Arms, 1775).

Rule M4 — Warn and Appeal to Fellow Citizens (teams; Revere Network)
Public appeals and open letters seeking consensus.

Rule M5 — Peaceful Pressure as the Last Admonition (teams; lesser magistrates)
Teams: lawful boycotts and lawsuits.
Lesser magistrates: refusal to enforce unlawful acts and legislative interposition.
"As the last peaceable admonition" (1775).

Rule M6 — A Lawful Civil Body Acts, with the People's Consent (lesser magistrates only)
Action by recorded vote, in public, within the body's own office, "by Authority of the good People" (1776).

Rule M7 — Count the Cost (lesser magistrates only)
A published cost-and-consequence assessment before any escalation (00F).
"We have counted the cost of this contest" (1775).

Rule M8 — Defensive Force Only Against Violence Actually Offered (lawful authority only)
Force only in defense, only by lawful authority (for Kansas, Kansas Constitution Article 8, Section 4; United States Constitution Article I, Section 10).
Never by private persons (00L Rule L13).
"Against violence actually offered, we have taken up arms" (1775).

Rule M9 — Declared Limits and Exit (lesser magistrates only)
Every interposition states its end condition in advance (00A).
"We shall lay them down when hostilities shall cease on the part of the aggressors" (1775).

Rule M10 — Separation, Last of All (lawful representative body only)
The final remedy, only after the record shows every prior step exhausted.


V. TEAMS STOP AT STEP FIVE

Rule M11 — Team Limit
Teams act only at Steps 1 through 5.
Teams never act as a government, never claim official authority, and never use force.
Teams may petition lesser magistrates to act at Step 6.


VI. THE REVERE NETWORK

Rule M12 — Confirmed Alerts Only
An alert travels the Revere Network only when a failed smoke test is rerun and confirmed by a second team.

Rule M13 — Facts Only
An alert carries the test card, the data, and the evidence. It calls only for Steps 1 through 5.

Rule M14 — Public and Correctable
Every alert is logged publicly. A false alarm is retracted publicly.

Rule M15 — Human Push
An AI may prepare an alert; a person sends it.
