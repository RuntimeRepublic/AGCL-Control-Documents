00K — Citizen Supremacy and Border Integrity Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Draft (not adopted)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-11-15
Supersedes: none
Scope: All AGCL-governed nations, states, borders, and foreign work programs


I. PURPOSE

This Control affirms the supremacy of citizens in their own labor market and the duty of every government to secure its border.

A government exists to protect.
Protection comes before tribute.
A border that is not controlled is not defended.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Citizens come first in the labor market of their own nation.

Every nation may defend that supremacy.
Every government is tested on the border powers it actually holds.


III. DEFINITIONS

Citizen Supremacy
The priority of a nation's citizens in its own labor market.

Foreign Work Program
Any program that authorizes non-citizens to work in a nation's labor market.

Invasion
For AGCL purposes, any movement of people across a border that the responsible government does not control.
(Current treatment: courts have not adopted this definition. See Section VIII.)

Border Test
The published, measurable smoke test of border control under 00L.


IV. CITIZEN SUPREMACY RULES

Rule K1 — Supremacy Affirmed
Every nation may defend the supremacy of its citizens in its labor market.

Rule K2 — Relationship to Point 06
For the United States, Point 06 (Labor Sovereignty) governs and terminates federal work-visa programs.
This Control adds requirements; it does not soften Point 06.

Rule K3 — Conditions on Any Foreign Work Program
Until Point 06 is in force, and in any nation where a foreign work program exists, the program must:
1. Meet every AGCL Control (000, 00A–00M).
2. Run under a 4-month authorization window (00A).
3. Pass a weekly public smoke test (00L).

Rule K4 — Automatic End on Failure
A failed weekly smoke test ends the program automatically.
New entries stop immediately.
Workers already admitted finish their current term (00D: no one is trapped).

Rule K5 — Zero Cost to Citizens
No citizen shall spend money or make a phone call to report or stop abuse of a foreign work program.
Monitoring runs through the public ledger (00G).
Program fees paid by sponsoring employers fund all enforcement.
Every complaint opens a case automatically (00L).


V. BORDER INTEGRITY RULES

Rule K6 — Secure Borders Required
A nation that cannot control its border fails the AGCL test.

Rule K7 — Each Level Tested on Its Own Powers
The nation is tested on securing the border.
Each state is tested on its duty to repel invasion and to support border protection under the powers it holds.
(Kansas: Kansas Constitution Article 8, Section 4; K.S.A. 48-203; K.S.A. 48-238. United States: Article I, Section 10, Clause 3; Article IV, Section 4.)

Rule K8 — Tribute Follows Protection
A government that fails its border test has not met the condition on which tribute is owed.
The remedy proceeds through 00I and 00L, never by violence.


VI. ENFORCEMENT

Failure of any rule in this Control triggers the 00I remedy order through a citizen trigger under 00L.


VII. SCRIPTURAL FOUNDATION (whole stories)

1 Samuel 25 — David and Nabal.
David's men protected Nabal's shepherds: "They were a wall unto us both by night and day" (25:16).
David asked for his due at shearing time (25:4–8). Nabal refused (25:10–11).
David set out for vengeance; Abigail stopped him (25:33) and brought the payment (25:18, 27).
God judged Nabal (25:38).
Teaching: protection earns the dues; the dues are owed; the remedy is lawful, not vengeance.

Romans 13:1–7.
Authority is "the minister of God to thee for good" (13:4).
"For this cause pay ye tribute also... Render therefore to all their dues" (13:6–7).
Teaching: tribute is owed for the function actually performed.


VIII. CURRENT TREATMENT AND PATH

Current treatment: courts have not adopted the AGCL definition of invasion, and federal work-visa programs remain in law.
Path: enact Point 06; state action under Article I, Section 10 backed by published border tests; or constitutional amendment.


IX. FOOTNOTES

Red team: weekly automatic shutdowns are harsh on employers and lawful workers, and a broad definition of invasion could justify force against unarmed people.
Blue team: the wind-down protects lawful workers; the border test is published and measurable; every remedy runs through due process under 00L, not a governor's say-so.


X. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Executive orders
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules


XI. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- Point 06 — Labor Sovereignty (adds to it)
- 00A, 00D, 00G, 00I, 00L


XII. DECLARATION

A nation that will not guard its gate has not earned the toll.
