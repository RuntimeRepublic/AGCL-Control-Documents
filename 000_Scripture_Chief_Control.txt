000 — Scripture Chief Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Draft (not adopted)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-11-15
Supersedes: none
Scope: All AGCL Controls, Points, doctrine documents, lessons, and rulings


I. PURPOSE

This Control establishes Scripture as the chief control over the AGCL system and sets the method by which Scripture is cited.

God makes the rules.
Rulers are ministers, not owners.
Drift is the first enemy of liberty.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Every authority traces to God, and every rule is tested against His Word read whole.

"Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's" (Matthew 22:21). Caesar's domain is inside God's rules, not beside them.


III. DEFINITIONS

Chief Control
The control every other AGCL document is read under.

Whole-Story Citation
Citing a passage with its full narrative, conditions, and outcome, never one line lifted out.

Ruling
The founder's recorded decision on a disputed reading, issued by the method in Section VI.

Witness
An economist, historian, theologian, or statesman whose work agrees with a rule. A witness is never an authority.


IV. RULES

Rule S1 — Scripture Is the Chief Control
Every AGCL Control and Point is read under Scripture.
Scripture is quoted from the King James Version.

Rule S2 — Three Domains, Family First
Family, civil government, and church are each legitimate domains under God.
Family is instituted first (Genesis 2:24); government's sword next (Genesis 9:6); then the worshipping assembly.
No domain may absorb another.

Rule S3 — Rulings on Disputed Readings
Where a reading is disputed, the founder issues a Ruling by the method in Section VI.
Every Ruling is recorded, dated, and versioned under 00J.
Until a Ruling exists, the reading is marked pending.

Rule S4 — Whole-Story and Whole-System Citation
Scripture is cited as whole stories, never one aspect lifted out and the rest left behind.
A practice drawn from Scripture is cited with its purpose, the consent by which it was adopted, its limits, and its reset.
A tax cited without its method of approval and who consented is returned in review.
Israel's civil laws are cited for their general equity as a whole system (Westminster Confession 19.4).

Rule S5 — Witnesses, Not Authorities
Economists, historians, and theologians are cited as witnesses who agree, never as authorities.
The Doctrinal Foundations document remains subordinate to the Controls.
Scripture is not subordinate; it is the chief control.

Rule S6 — Classification of Every Scripture Link
Every link is classified as exactly one of:
Command — the text directly commands it.
Principle — the text states a principle the rule applies.
Precedent — the text records an example the rule follows.
Analogy — the text governs something else; the rule reasons by comparison.
Modern design — no scriptural basis; an engineering choice.
No link is described with stronger wording than its class allows.
Every link with a counter-text names it.

Rule S7 — Correction by Lawful Process
Unlawful rule is corrected through lawful process and consensus, not private violence (00L).
Lesser magistrates may hold a higher magistrate accountable by the ordered steps of 00M.


V. SCRIPTURAL FOUNDATION (whole stories)

Genesis 2:17; 3:3 — The first drift.
God told Adam not to eat of the tree; Eve reported, "neither shall ye touch it."
Teaching: adding to a rule is drift (Rule S4; 00J).

1 Samuel 25 — David, Nabal and Abigail.
Protection earned the dues; Nabal refused; Abigail stopped David's vengeance and paid; God judged.
Teaching: dues follow protection; the remedy is lawful (Rule S7).

2 Samuel 24 — David's census.
The king counted fighting men; judgment followed; the king then refused to take Araunah's land as a gift: "neither will I offer burnt offerings unto the LORD my God of that which doth cost me nothing" (24:24).
Teaching: even the king pays full price for private land.

Matthew 22:15–22 — Render unto Caesar.
A trap question; the coin bears Caesar's image; man bears God's image (Genesis 1:27).
Teaching: God makes the rules (Rule S1).

Romans 13:1–7 — The minister of God.
Authority is "the minister of God to thee for good"; "For this cause pay ye tribute also."
Teaching: authority is defined by its function.


VI. HERMENEUTICAL METHOD (for Rulings)

1. Read the whole story, with what comes before and after.
2. Compare Scripture with Scripture.
3. Name every counter-text.
4. Classify the link (Rule S6).
5. State the Ruling in one sentence, with its date and version (00J).
6. Record what would change the Ruling.


VII. FOOTNOTES

Red team: critics will say a Scripture-based framework cannot govern a pluralist nation, and that a single founder's Rulings concentrate interpretive power.
Blue team: the AGCL governs government's structure, not anyone's conscience; every Ruling is public, dated, and versioned, names its counter-texts, and can be challenged; and the founders themselves read authority through Scripture.


VIII. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by any other AGCL Control, doctrine document, emergency, or administrative act.


IX. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- All AGCL Controls (00A–00M) and Points
- The Doctrinal Foundations document
- All lessons and Rulings


X. DECLARATION

The law is king because God is King.
