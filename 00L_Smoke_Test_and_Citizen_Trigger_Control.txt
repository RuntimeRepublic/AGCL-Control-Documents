00L — Smoke Test and Citizen Trigger Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Draft (not adopted)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-11-15
Supersedes: none
Scope: All AGCL Controls, Points, programs, and authorities


I. PURPOSE

This Control requires every AGCL standard to carry a cited smoke test and gives every citizen a way to trigger enforcement through due process.

Citizens grant enumerated powers with strings attached.
A standard without a test is a suggestion.
A test without a citizen trigger is a report no one must read.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Every power is tested. Every citizen can pull the trigger. Every remedy is lawful.

Correction comes by due process and consensus, never by violence.


III. DEFINITIONS

Test Card
The required record of an AGCL standard's smoke test (Section IV).

Smoke Test
A measurable, published pass/fail check run on official public data.

Citizen Trigger
A citizen's filing that a smoke test has failed.


IV. TEST CARD REQUIREMENT

Rule L1 — Every Standard Carries a Test Card
Each AGCL Control and Point must state:
1. Standard (one sentence)
2. Smoke test (pass/fail check)
3. Data source (official, public, cited; 00G)
4. Frequency (weekly unless stated)
5. Threshold (the exact failing number or condition)
6. Runner (automated and public; anyone may rerun it)
7. Remedy (the 00I step that follows failure)
8. Current treatment (how courts and governments treat it today, with sources)
9. Path (legislation, amendment, or evidence and petitions)
10. Scripture (whole-story citation, where applicable)

Rule L2 — Incomplete Without a Test Card
A standard without a test card is marked incomplete.

Rule L3 — Thresholds Are Versioned
Every threshold change is a recorded amendment under 00J.


V. CITIZEN TRIGGER

Rule L4 — Any Citizen May File
Standing is presumed. No lawyer is required.

Rule L5 — Zero Cost
Filing costs the citizen nothing. No fee. No phone call.

Rule L6 — Evidence
A failed public smoke test, or a rerun of it with the data attached.

Rule L7 — Automatic Docketing
Every filing receives a public case number at once (00I Rule I9).

Rule L8 — Burden on Government
The government must prove compliance within 14 days.
Silence equals failure (00A).

Rule L9 — Neutral Review
A citizen jury or neutral adjudicator decides the facts (Point 08).
The model is the elders at the gate.

Rule L10 — Remedy Order
Remedies follow 00I: invalidation, restitution, suspension, removal, referral.

Rule L11 — No Retaliation
Retaliation against a filer is a separate violation.

Rule L12 — Priority for the Weak
Widows, orphans, and others without means are heard first.

Rule L13 — No Self-Help
No filer may take a remedy by force.
Unlawful taxation is corrected through lawful process and consensus, not war.


VI. DUE PROCESS ON BOTH SIDES

The citizen receives notice, a hearing, a decision with reasons, and an appeal.
The government receives the same opportunity to show the data was wrong.
Review covers facts and scope of remedy and may not delay enforcement (00I Rule I10).


VII. SCRIPTURAL FOUNDATION (whole stories)

Genesis 2:17; 3:3 — The first drift.
God told Adam not to eat of the tree. Eve reported, "neither shall ye touch it."
Teaching: adding to a rule is drift. Every change is recorded (00J; Rule L3).

Genesis 4:15–26 — The first city and the first unlimited vengeance.
God set a mark on Cain to limit vengeance (4:15). Cain built the first city (4:17).
Lamech boasted of vengeance "seventy and sevenfold" (4:23–24).
Then men began to call upon the name of Jehovah (4:26).
Teaching: without a lawful limit, vengeance grows without bound (Rule L13).

Exodus 22:22–24; Deuteronomy 27:19 — Widows and orphans.
"If thou afflict them in any wise... I will kill you with the sword."
"Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow."
Teaching: the weak are heard first (Rule L12).

Ruth 4:1–11; Amos 5:12, 15 — Judgment at the gate.
Boaz settled a land and family matter before the elders at the gate.
"Establish judgment in the gate."
Teaching: neutral review in public (Rule L9).

Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10–11; Hosea 5:10 — Landmarks.
"Remove not the old landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless: For their redeemer is mighty; he shall plead their cause with thee."
"The princes of Judah were like them that remove the bound."
Teaching: rulers may not move the boundary; the fatherless have an advocate.

1 Samuel 25 — David, Nabal and Abigail.
Protection earned the dues; Nabal refused; David set out for vengeance; Abigail stopped him and paid; God judged.
Teaching: the dues are real and the remedy is lawful (Rules L10, L13).

Luke 3:12–14 — Correction of tax collectors and soldiers.
Tax collectors asked John what to do: "Exact no more than that which is appointed you."
Soldiers asked the same: "Do violence to no man, neither accuse any falsely; and be content with your wages."
Teaching: overreach is corrected at its source, without violence.

Luke 19:1–10 — Zacchaeus.
A chief tax collector met Jesus and said, "if I have taken any thing from any man by false accusation, I restore him fourfold."
Teaching: restitution corrects unlawful taking (00I Rule I6).

Matthew 17:24–27 — The temple tax.
Jesus taught that the children are free, "Notwithstanding, lest we should offend them," and paid.
Teaching: a disputed tax is not a cause for violence; work through it without giving offense.

Matthew 22:15–22 — Render unto Caesar.
A trap question. The coin bears Caesar's image; man bears God's image (Gen. 1:27).
Teaching: God makes the rules; Caesar's domain is inside them.

Matthew 26:52 — Peter's sword.
"Put up again thy sword into his place: for all they that take the sword shall perish with the sword."
Teaching: correction by process, not the sword (Rule L13).


VIII. FOOTNOTES

Red team: presumed standing and automatic docketing could flood the system with filings; critics read Romans 13 and Matthew 22 as a plain duty to pay.
Blue team: a filing needs a failed public test, not an opinion; the government gets a fair hearing; and read whole, these passages correct overreach through repentance, restitution and lawful judgment, never rebellion.


IX. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Executive orders
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay


X. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs every AGCL Control and Point.
It works with 00A, 00G, 00I, 00J, and Point 08.


XI. DECLARATION

Power granted with strings attached must be tested, and the citizen must be able to pull the string.
