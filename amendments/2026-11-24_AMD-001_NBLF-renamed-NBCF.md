## Explicit amendment (J3)
1. The framework currently called **NBLF** is renamed **NBCF, Node-Based Constitutional Framework**. The AGCL is the United States of America's NBCF.
2. The name **NBLF** is reassigned to a new framework, **Node-Based Legal Framework**, covering courts, police, prosecutors, defense, clerks, the bar and court experts.
3. The frameworks become: NBCF (constitutional), NBLF (legal), NBBF (budget), NBEF (education).
